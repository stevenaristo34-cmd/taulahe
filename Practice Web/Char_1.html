STORY

Ethan Winters was an American mutant trained as a systems engineer. One of many mutant victims of biological weaponry, Winters was infected with a weaponized species of "Mold" during the 2017 Dulvey incident, caused by Eveline, an E-001 bioweapon created by The Connections and HCF. Following Eveline's death by his hands, Winters and his wife Mia were rescued by Chris Redfield of the Bioterrorism Security Assessment Alliance.

Fearing the Winters were now The Connections's targets, they were placed under witness protection and relocated to Eastern Europe. Since the Baker Incident, Winters received military training, and have their daughter Rosemary Winters with his wife. However, this put them in Miranda's crosshair, as Rosemary was considered to be a candidate for her daughter Eva's vessel. By February 2021, Winters was placed in the Village, where Rosemary was held by Miranda. Eventually, Winters successfully rescued Rosemary, but gave up his life to save his family from Miranda's clutches.
